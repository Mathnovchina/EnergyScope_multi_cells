Major comments
▶The introduction and literature review are concise and convincing.
▶The results are presented beautifully in detail.
▶The model description is not so clear by the structure and the content. It could
be decomposed into three sections, stylized facts, model description, data
specification and parameter calibration.
▶The model description only shows 7 equations, which are not linked together. I
cannot understand the model mechanism clearly but through guessing, especially
section 3.3.2 end-use demand in energy services.
▶It is not clear which part of the models are borrowed, which are innovated by the
author.
▶Exogenous end-use demand is a strong assumption, need more discussion as a
limitation. Introducing additional scenarios, high/low energy demand in 2035,
distributions?
